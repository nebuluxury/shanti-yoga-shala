Studio Policies & Procedures

A calm, respectful space for everyone

A few simple guidelines keep the shala safe, peaceful, and welcoming. Thank you for helping us protect the practice for every student in the room.

Before your first class

All clients entering the studio are required to sign our updated liability waiver. Arrive a few minutes early for your first visit so we can welcome you and get you settled.

Arrival & the space

  • Please use the white cubicles to store your shoes and keys.
  • You're welcome to bring your phone in - silenced, please.
  • Classes are capped at 14 students, with mats placed along the floor markings so everyone has proper spacing.

Health & wellness

Please do not attend class if you're showing symptoms of illness or allergies. If you've been exposed to a contagious illness, kindly wait 10 days before returning. If you must miss a class due to illness, we'll gladly waive the class deduction - just notify us directly by phone or email.

Cancellations

All class cancellations must be made at least 2 hours before the class begins. We watch this closely to keep our small classes available for everyone who wants a mat.

No-show fees

A $15 no-show fee applies to members; pass holders will have one class deducted. Illness-related absences won't incur a charge when you notify the studio directly.
