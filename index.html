What "shala" means

A home for the practice - and the people in it

Shala means home. That's exactly what we set out to build: a quiet, welcoming space in Palm Beach Gardens dedicated to recovering the essence of yoga - a traditional philosophy and practice that belongs to everyone.

We keep our classes small on purpose. With no more than fourteen mats in the room, our teachers actually see you: your breath, your alignment, the small adjustments that turn a pose into a practice. Come flexible, come stiff, come as a beginner or a lifelong student. You'll leave lighter than you arrived.
